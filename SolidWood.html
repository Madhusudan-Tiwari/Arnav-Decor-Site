Solid Wood

About Solid Wood

Solid Wood Doors are crafted using traditional rail-and-stile construction, a time-tested method known for its strength, durability, and classic appeal. Each door is built to offer long-lasting performance while preserving the natural beauty and character of real wood.

The frames are formed from single, solid sections of wood, allowing the material to retain its inherent grain, texture, and structural integrity. This construction enhances stability, ensures a robust feel, and highlights the authentic qualities of solid timber, making these doors a reliable and elegant choice for both residential and commercial interiors.

Key Features

  • Durable wear-resistant surface
  • Realistic wood grain finish
  • Available in plank sizes of 4x8 and 3x6
  • Low maintenance
  • Ideal for high traffic areas
  • Cost-effective alternative to hardwood
