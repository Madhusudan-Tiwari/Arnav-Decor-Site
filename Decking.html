Decking

Stable, long-lasting, and well-finished structure.

About Decking Flooring

We offer a versatile range of high-quality decking solutions suitable for both indoor and outdoor applications. Designed to combine durability with visual appeal, our decking systems feature advanced locking technology and slip-resistant surfaces for enhanced safety and performance.

To ensure a smooth, end-to-end experience, we also provide professional installation services. Our installation process uses a robust aluminum substructure with timber inserts, reinforced with polypropylene (PP) blocks to improve strength and longevity. Each deck panel is precisely cut and securely fixed to the base, resulting in a stable, long-lasting, and well-finished structure.

Key Features

  • Durable Constructio:n Designed for long-lasting performance in both indoor and outdoor environments.
  • Anti-Skid Surface: Provides enhanced safety with slip-resistant properties.
  • Advanced Locking System: Features an innovative clip-lock mechanism for secure and seamless installation.
  • Weather Resistant: Built to withstand moisture, temperature changes, and outdoor conditions.
  • Robust Substructure: Installed on an aluminum framework reinforced with polypropylene (PP) blocks for added strength.
  • Professional Installation: Precision cutting and secure fixing ensure a stable, high-quality finished deck.
