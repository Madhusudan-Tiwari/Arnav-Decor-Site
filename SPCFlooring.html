SPC Flooring

Excellent wear resistance, long-lasting performance, and a detailed surface texture.

About SPC Flooring

SPC (Stone Polymer Composite) Flooring is designed for strength, stability, and style. Built from high-performance composite materials, it offers exceptional durability while maintaining a natural appearance. With an easy click-lock installation system and a surface made to resist wear and abrasion, SPC flooring delivers reliable performance with a premium finish.

Key Features

  • Rigid Core Construction: Stone-polymer core provides exceptional strength, stability, and impact resistance.
  • Water-Resistant Performance: Ideal for areas prone to moisture such as kitchens and high-traffic spaces.
  • High Wear & Abrasion Resistance: Durable surface layer protects against scratches, stains, and daily wear.
  • Easy Click-Lock Installation Precision locking system allows fast, glue-free installation.
  • Dimensional Stability Maintains shape and performance under temperature and humidity variations.
  • Low Maintenance Simple cleaning and minimal upkeep for long-term use.
